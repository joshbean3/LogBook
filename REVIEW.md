## Hand checks

1. Plan → New block: with sessions ahead the button reads "Replace N sessions" and the line above it says what happens. Tap it: you land on the new block's first week, toast "… N sessions replaced." with Undo. Tap Undo: "Put back as it was."; the old block and its sessions are back.
2. Plan → the block card → the plan → Delete plan: toast "Plan deleted · Undo". Reload at once: the plan is gone, past Done runs stay. Delete again and tap Undo: it comes back.
3. Plan week: pull the header down slowly: the week and the block card come down together; "Pull for the month" appears in its own space, turns to "Let go for the month" with a tick; let go → month. Tap ⓘ under the grid for the legend.
4. Swipe a session row left halfway: its name stays put next to the Move pane.
5. Open Friday's Easy 4: OUT THE DOOR 7:30 (as Today's hero). Edit → 4.5 mi → Save: the title reads Easy 4.5.
6. Mark a missed run Done (no Strava): Plan's header, Progress (bar with a hairline), the week sheet and Share all count its miles; Share leads with Stamp showing only the distance.
7. Share the day on a rest day with an entry: Day card titled Rest with the entry; no "0.00", no "—'—", no week card when the week has no miles.
8. Progress: press the chart and slide: the numbers equal the bar under the finger; let go: they spring back. Tap this week's bar → a session → Back returns to the week.
9. A new account: Progress shows bars with no numbers; Today's week ring → "Nothing planned yet" + Plan a week.
