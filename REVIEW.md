## 2. What a reviewer can verify by hand (port 8803)

Open `http://localhost:8803/today` on a phone-sized viewport (iPhone 14, 390 wide) after signing in.

1. **Migration ran.** `sqlite3 apps/api/data/test.db "pragma table_info(todos)"` lists `priority INTEGER default 0`, `due TEXT`, `bucket TEXT default 'today'`, `position INTEGER default 0`; `select count(*) from __drizzle_migrations` is 5. Migrations apply on boot, nothing to run by hand.
2. **API shape.** In the browser console `await (await fetch('/api/todos')).json()` returns rows with `priority`, `due`, `bucket`, `position`. The list is ordered by `position`.
3. **API priority validation.** `fetch('/api/todos/<id>', { method: 'PATCH', headers: { 'content-type': 'application/json' }, body: JSON.stringify({ priority: 3 }) })` returns **422** `{"error":"priority: Invalid input","code":"validation"}`; with `{ priority: 2 }` it returns the row with `priority: 2`. `PUT /api/todos/order` with an id longer than 64 chars is also 422.
4. **API when.** PATCH `{ bucket: 'later', due: '2026-10-21' }` returns `bucket: 'later', due: '2026-10-21'`; then PATCH `{ bucket: 'today' }` returns `due: null`; PATCH `{ due: '2026-10-20' }` alone on a Today row still returns `due: null` (a date only lives on a Later item). PATCH `{ bucket: 'later' }` with no date, or POST `{ bucket: 'later' }` with no date, returns `due` = tomorrow (Later always has a day).
5. **API order.** `PUT /api/todos/order` with `{ order: ['<idA>', '<idB>'] }` returns the full list with A at position 0 and B at 1. `POST /api/todos` with `{ text, date, priority: 2, bucket: 'later', due }` creates a Later item at position max+1.
6. **Quick-add marks.** Tap into the empty "Add a to-do" field: a caps hint `!! · FRI · SOMEDAY` appears beside it (the input's `aria-describedby` points at it). Type `Call the bank fri !!` and press Enter: the row appears under **Later** with two white dots and `OCT 16` (next Friday) at the right; while typing, the hint reads `HIGH · OCT 16`. `Plan the week !` lands in Today with one dot; `Read it !!!` is high; `Ship it!` (no space) keeps its `!` and no priority. `Sort the garage someday` lands under Someday. `Book the dentist tomorrow` lands under Later with `TOMORROW`; `Call back later` lands under Later with `TOMORROW` too. The Add button is 44px tall; at 320 wide the hint wraps under the field and the field keeps at least 160px.
7. **Folds.** The **Later (n)** row shows the next two *distinct* due dates on the right (`OCT 14 · OCT 16`, never `OCT 14 · OCT 14`); tapping it expands the rows, sorted by date. **Someday (n)** and **Completed (n)** fold the same way; all start collapsed.
8. **Today sort.** Today's open rows are high first, then medium, then none, then hand order. The stat strip's "To-dos N open" counts Today's list only (Later/Someday are not counted).
9. **Sheet.** Tap a to-do's name: the sheet has the name (44px tall, nothing auto-focused), Note, a **Priority** segmented (None / • Medium / •• High; the dots are decorative, VoiceOver reads "Medium"), **When** chips (Today / Later / Someday), and for open items in a list of two or more an **Order** row with 44px Move up / Move down and "n of m". Pick Later: a date field appears, pre-filled with tomorrow, `min` tomorrow, and it takes focus. Save (the sheet's only blue) is disabled until something changes. Set High + Later + a date and Save: the row moves to Later with two dots and the date. Open it again, pick Today, Save: it returns to Today and the API row shows `due: null`.
10. **Overdue surfacing.** PATCH a Later item to `due: '2026-10-13'` (today) or earlier: it appears in Today's list; a past date shows `DUE OCT 12` caps at the right; today's date shows nothing extra.
11. **Press-and-hold reorder.** On a touch device (or Playwright with `hasTouch` + CDP touch), press and hold a Today row for 450ms (350ms does not lift): a haptic fires (where supported), the row lifts (surface-2 background, hairline, class `is-lifted`; the group gets `is-dragging` and the blue/red underlays are hidden); drag it vertically and release: the order changes, `/api/todos` shows new `position` values 0..n for that group, and the order survives a reload. **The drop adopts the tier it landed in**: drag a plain row above the high rows and it gets two dots and `priority: 2`; drag a high row below a medium one and it becomes medium. In Later, a row dropped among another day's rows takes that day. Hold and release without moving: nothing opens. A short tap on the name (no hold) still opens the sheet. Completed rows never lift.
11b. **Non-drag reorder.** In the sheet, Move up / Move down step the row one place (same tier rule; the sheet's Priority segmented follows). With the keyboard, focus a row's name (Tab) and press Alt+ArrowUp / Alt+ArrowDown. Both persist through `PUT /todos/order`.
12. **Swipe.** Swipe right past 56px completes (blue check underneath, tick haptic at the threshold, the row folds away and appears under Completed); swipe left deletes (red X) and a "Deleted" toast with **Undo** appears (as does the sheet's Delete); Undo clears `deletedOn` and the row returns. Tapping the square mark fills it, draws the check, then folds the row after the celebration. The mark's label reads the date with the item ("Bank, Oct 16", "Water the plants, from Oct 11").
13. **Carried items.** A Today to-do created on an earlier day and still open shows its origin date as a small pill (`OCT 11`) at the right, as before. A Later item surfaced on its day shows neither the pill nor a date.
13b. **Empty states.** With nothing in Today but items in Later or Someday: "Nothing for today." and an **Open** button that expands the Later fold (Someday when Later is empty). With nothing anywhere but something completed: "All clear for today." With nothing at all: "Nothing on the list. What you don't finish rolls to tomorrow."
13c. **Provisional rows.** Throttle `POST /api/todos` (DevTools, or Playwright `page.route` with a 2.5s delay), add a to-do and drag it to the top before the POST returns: it stays where it was put, `/api/todos` shows it at position 0 of the group with the adopted priority once the id arrives (the queued `PUT /todos/order` and `PATCH` replay with the real id), and a reload agrees. Hold the drag past the 2.5s so the POST lands *during* the drag: the row stays on screen and lifted through the naming (its React key is the id it was born with), and the drop still lands it first. A row whose POST resolves during someone else's drag is on screen after the drop.
13d. **Fully offline.** Go offline (DevTools, or Playwright `context.setOffline(true)`), add a to-do, drag it to the top, go back online: the outbox replays the create, learns the real id from the reply, rewrites it into the queued `PUT /todos/order` and tier `PATCH`, and `/api/todos` shows the row first in Today with `priority: 2`; a reload agrees. The outbox persists across a reload while offline and relearns the id on replay.
14. **Widths.** At 320 and 390 there is no horizontal overflow (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= clientWidth`), rows are 44px, dates and dots do not wrap mid-word.
15. **Typecheck.** `pnpm -r --filter @logbook/shared --filter @logbook/api --filter @logbook/web exec tsc --noEmit -p .` passes; `pnpm --filter @logbook/web build` passes.
16. **Desktop.** Right-click on a row opens the browser's context menu (only a touch long-press is suppressed).

## 3. Left out or not finished

- Quick-add parsing is deliberately small: a space then `!`/`!!`/`!!!`, `today`, `tomorrow`/`tmrw`, weekday names (full or 3-letter), `later` (= tomorrow), `someday`. No "next week", no explicit dates, no times.
- Overdue Later items surface into Today in the client (`inToday`); the server does not rewrite their bucket. Their row shows `DUE <date>` in ink, not a status colour.
- A drop or a Move adopts the tier it lands in (priority in Today and Someday, the day in Later), so there is no snap-back. In Later the tier is the day only: a same-day drop above a higher-priority row settles below it (the list is date, then priority, then hand order), and a drop among another day's rows takes that day but keeps its priority. Rows created before migration 0004 may be Later without a date; they sort after dated ones and are not re-dated until edited (a PATCH never re-dates a row on its own; only a move into Later without a date gets tomorrow).
- Provisional rows, online or offline, are handled by the outbox (see the file table): the only remaining gap is a create the server *rejects* (validation), whose queued dependents are then sent with the provisional id and dropped as 404s, which is the outbox's existing rule for rejected items.
- The post-add order write sends only the moved row's group (the `PUT` the drag queued), never the whole cache.
- The Undo toast lasts as long as `ToastProvider` gives any Undo (6.5s; not my file).
- Reordering does not use motion's `Reorder` component. It auto-scrolls the page whenever the finger is within 50px of the bottom of `body`, and with `html, body, #root { height: 100% }` (base.css, not mine to edit) `body` is always one viewport tall, so the page ran away on every drag (verified: `window.scrollBy` calls from `autoScrollIfNeeded` growing 3 → 554px). `TodoGroup` applies the same swap rule by hand (a row swaps with its neighbour once its edge passes the neighbour's centre, measured with `onLayoutMeasure`). Each row is one moving `motion.div` that carries both gestures (`drag` is `'x'` for the swipe and flips to `'y'` once the hold lifts it, on the same gesture session; `dragDirectionLock` keeps a sideways start a swipe and a still-then-vertical start a reorder); the swipe underlay sits in a static wrap. A nested drag element inside a `layout` row was tried first and left the lifted row's contents displaced by its old slot (a stale child measurement in motion's projection tree). The cost of the hand-rolled version: no auto-scroll while dragging; the groups are short lists inside a page, so a drag stays within the viewport.
- The lifted row uses a hairline and the control surface, no drop shadow, so it stays inside the brand's "no shadows on the canvas" rule; it does not scale.
- Haptics come from `haptic()` (Capacitor on iOS, `navigator.vibrate` elsewhere); headless Chromium reports nothing, so haptics were not machine-verified.
- No automated tests were added; verification was done with a Playwright script against port 8803 (screenshots in the report).
