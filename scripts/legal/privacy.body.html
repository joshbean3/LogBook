Orby is a training, habits, goals and journal app with an AI assistant. This policy says what Orby collects, why, who receives it and what you can do about it. It covers Orby on iPhone and Orby's website.

The short version

  • Your data is yours. It is never sold and never used for advertising.
  • Nothing goes to the AI assistant's provider until you allow it, and you choose what it may use.
  • What Orby reads from Apple Health is never sent to AI, never used for advertising and never stored in iCloud.
  • You can export everything and delete your account in the app at any time.

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name, if you give one.
  • Your password. We store only a scrambled form of it (a salted hash). We cannot read it.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in on each device.
  • The dates your account was made and your email address was confirmed.
  • Your answers to the permission questions in the app, with the date of each answer.

What you enter

  • Your training plan and sessions, what you mark done, effort notes and lifts you log.
  • Your training status, such as sick, injured, resting or traveling, when you set one.
  • Your habits and the days you check them, your wake time and your morning routine.
  • Your to-dos and goals, and what you write when you close a goal.
  • Your journal entries and learnings.
  • Links you save, with your notes and tags.
  • Your settings.

Some of this can say something about your health, for example a journal entry or a status of sick or injured. We treat it with the same care as health readings.

The assistant

  • What you type to the assistant or the plan builder, and any photo or PDF you attach.
  • Changes the assistant proposes to your plan, to-dos or goals, with the request that led to them, so you can approve or undo them.

Your chats with the assistant are kept on your phone, not on our server. Signing out or deleting your account clears them from the phone.

Apple Health, only if you connect it

If you connect Apple Health, Orby reads exactly three things and saves them to your Orby account so it can show them to you:

  • Sleep: the start, end and stage of each stretch of sleep.
  • Heart rate variability.
  • Resting heart rate.

With each reading we keep the time zone, the model of the device that recorded it (for example "Watch7,1") and the identifier of the app that wrote it to Apple Health. We do not collect the name of your device. Orby reads up to one year of history when you connect. Orby never writes to Apple Health and reads nothing else from it.

Technical records

  • Server logs: the time, which part of the app was called, whether it worked and, at most, a scrambled account number. Logs do not hold what you wrote.
  • A security log: sign-ins, failed sign-ins, password and email changes, your permission answers and account deletion, each with the time and a scrambled account number. It holds nothing you wrote.
  • Your IP address, used briefly to slow down repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts.
  • Error reports: if the app fails, it sends the error, the screen it happened on, the app version and the type of device. An error report holds none of your content and is not linked to your account.

What stays on your phone

  • Reminders. They are scheduled on the phone and never reach our server.
  • Drafts, changes waiting to sync and your view settings.
  • Cards you share. Orby makes the image on your phone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add it to your photos.
  • Camera and photos. Orby sees only the photo or file you choose to attach.

What we do not collect

No location. No contacts. No advertising identifier. No analytics or tracking tools from other companies. Orby shows no ads.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the app: keep your plan, habits, goals, journal and settings and show them on your devices.
  • To show you your sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate.
  • To answer you when you use the assistant, the plan builder, the goal review, Remember when or the daily pick, if you have allowed it.
  • To send the mail you ask for: a link to confirm your address or to reset your password.
  • To keep the service working and safe: limits on sign-in attempts and on AI use, fixing errors, backups.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it, and do not let anyone else use it, to train AI models. We do not combine it with data from other companies.

Links

5. Your choices

  • AI. In Settings, Assistant access under Privacy has a switch for each kind of data the assistant may use. Turn one off and that data stops going to Claude from then on.
  • Apple Health. In Settings, open Apple Health under Data sources and tap Disconnect Apple Health. That stops the reading and deletes the readings saved to your account. You can also change what Orby may read in the iOS Settings app under Health.
  • Reminders, camera and photos. Change these in the iOS Settings app under Orby.
  • A copy of your data. In Settings, Import and export, then Export, saves one file with everything in your account.
  • Delete your account. In Settings, tap your email address under Account, then Delete account. That removes your account and everything in it from our database at once.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and copy your data: use Export in Settings, or ask us.
  • Correct it: edit it in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete it: delete your account in the app, or ask us.
  • Take back a permission: use the switches in Settings under Privacy, or disconnect Apple Health.
  • Ask which companies received your data. They are the four listed in section 4.

To ask, write to {{supportMailto}} from the email address of your account. We answer within 45 days and complete a deletion within 30 days. If we say no, you can reply to ask for a second look, and we will answer that within 45 days. If you are still unhappy, you can contact your state attorney general. We will never treat you worse for using these rights.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to the server, the database and the backups is limited to the publisher. On your phone, what Orby reads from Apple Health is kept in protected files that are left out of iCloud and device backups. No system is perfectly safe. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Age

Orby is for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Where your data is

Orby is offered in the United States. Our server is in {{dataCountry}}. The companies in section 4 are based in the United States and may process data there.

11. Tracking and "Do Not Track"

Orby does not track you across apps or websites, and no other company collects data about you through Orby over time. Because of that, Orby does not change how it works when a browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we post the new text here with a new date. If a change affects how your data is used, we tell you in the app before it takes effect. If we ever want to send more of your data to AI than you have allowed, the app asks you again first.
