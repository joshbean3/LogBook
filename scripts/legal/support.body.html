Short answers

I forgot my password

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?
  2. Enter your email address. We send you a link that works for 30 minutes.
  3. Open the link, choose a new password, then open Logbook and sign in.

No mail after a few minutes? Check the spam folder and the spelling of the address. If it still does not come, write to us.

Change my password

In Logbook, open Settings and tap your email address under Account. Tap Change password. Your other devices are signed out.

Delete my account

  1. In Logbook, open Settings and tap your email address under Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and everything in it are removed from our database at once. Copies in backups are gone within {{backupClearDays}} days. This cannot be undone, so export your data first if you want a copy.

Cannot get into the app? Write to us from the email address of the account and we will delete it for you.

Export my data

In Logbook, open Settings, then Import and export, then Export. Logbook saves one file with your plan, sessions, habits, to-dos, goals, journal, learnings, saved links and readings from Apple Health.

Disconnect Apple Health

  1. In Logbook, open Settings, then Apple Health under Data sources.
  2. Tap Disconnect Apple Health and confirm.

Logbook stops reading and deletes the sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate it saved to your account. Nothing changes in the Apple Health app. To change what Logbook may read, open the iOS Settings app, then Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Logbook.

Choose what the assistant can use

In Logbook, open Settings, then Assistant access under Privacy. Each switch covers one kind of data. With every switch off, nothing from your account goes to the assistant.

The assistant is AI and it can be wrong. If an answer looks wrong or unsafe, write to us and include what you asked.

Reminders do not show

Open the iOS Settings app, then Notifications, then Logbook, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Is Logbook medical advice?

No. Logbook is for fitness and wellness. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing your training, and if something feels wrong. In an emergency call 911.
