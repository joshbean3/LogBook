Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date this text takes effect

Draft. This text is waiting for approval and is not final yet.

This policy describes how Orby, published by legal name of the publisher, collects, uses and shares consumer health data. It is given under the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada law on consumer health data. Our Privacy Policy covers all other data.

1. The health data we collect

  • Sleep. The start, end and stage of each stretch of sleep.
  • Heart measurements. Heart rate variability and resting heart rate.
  • Training. Your training plan and sessions, effort notes and lifts you log.
  • Health status you tell us. A training status of sick or injured, and anything about your health that you write in a journal entry, a learning, a goal, a to-do or a message to the assistant.

2. Where it comes from

  • You, when you type it into Orby.
  • Apple Health on your iPhone, only if you connect it. Orby reads sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate, and nothing else.

3. Why we collect it and how we use it

  • To keep your training log, journal and goals and show them on your devices.
  • To show your sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate on Progress.
  • To answer you when you use the AI assistant, only with the data you have allowed it to use.
  • To keep the service working: backups and fixing errors.

We do not use consumer health data for advertising. We do not sell it.

4. The health data we share and who receives it

We share consumer health data only with the companies that process it for us, and only so they can provide their service to us:

CompanyHealth data it receives
Fly.io (server hosting)Everything listed in section 1.
Cloudflare R2 (backup storage)A copy of everything listed in section 1, kept for 5 days.
Anthropic (the AI model, Claude)Only with your permission: your training plan, sessions and training status, your goals and to-dos, what you type or attach to the assistant, and one past journal entry or learning a day. Never sleep, heart rate variability or resting heart rate.
Resend (email)None. It receives your email address only.

We have no affiliates. We do not share consumer health data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

5. Your rights and how to use them

  • Know and get a copy. You can confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data and get a copy of it. In Settings, use Import and export, then Export, or write to us.
  • Know who received it. You can ask for the list of companies your health data was shared with. It is the list in section 4.
  • Withdraw consent. Turn off the switches in Settings under Privacy to stop sharing with Anthropic. In Settings, open Apple Health under Data sources and tap Disconnect Apple Health to stop the reading and delete the saved readings.
  • Delete. In Settings, tap your email address under Account, then Delete account, or write to us. We delete your data from our database at once. It leaves snapshots and backups within 5 days. Anthropic deletes what it received within 30 days, or up to 2 years if it was flagged for breaking Anthropic's usage rules.

To make a request, write to support@example.com from the email address of your account. We answer within 45 days, and we complete a deletion within 30 days. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. We will answer the appeal in writing within 45 days. If the appeal is refused, you can complain to the Washington State Attorney General at www.atg.wa.gov or to the Nevada Attorney General at ag.nv.gov.

6. Collection by other companies

No other company collects consumer health data about you through Orby over time or across other websites or online services.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we post the new text here with a new date. If the change affects how your health data is used, we tell you in the app before it takes effect and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
