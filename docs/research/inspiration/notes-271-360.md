## 3. Patterns

Layout and navigation
- Floating glass tab bar (Home, Journal, Fitness, Biology) with a separate round "+" (296) or + centred in the bar (304). On scroll it collapses to just home + ask bar + "+" (298-303). Header collapses to a compact "Today" nav with calendar icon (298).
- Persistent "Ask [coach] anything" bar with mascot avatar sits just above the tab bar on every Home state (296, 299, 316); notification dot on the avatar when the coach has something new (299).
- Date is the page title with a chevron ("Today, September 3 v") and opens a calendar sheet (312). Detail pages repeat this pattern under their title (325, 347, 359).
- Detail pages = push with themed illustrated hero; drill-down items = bottom sheets with grabber and sticky compact header on scroll (329, 350).
- Sheets push sub-steps inside themselves with a back chevron (306 -> 314 -> 312), never a new full screen.
- Home is user-editable: "Edit Home" / "View All Categories" at the end (303).

Cards and components
- White rounded cards (~20px radius) on a very light lavender-grey background, soft large shadows, no borders; section headers are plain bold text outside cards (Stress & Energy, Nutrition, Health Monitor, Timeline).
- Stat tile: small grey icon + label header, hairline, big number + small grey unit (325, 347, 359). Metric tile: big value, status line with coloured icon (✓ Normal green, ↑ Higher blue, ↓ Lower orange), vertical range pill at right (301).
- Timeline rows: icon tile with corner score badge, title, timestamp, arrow (302-303). Badges colour-coded by domain (orange strain, blue sleep, purple food quality).
- Status chip ("Active / Until changed") as a first-class, time-bound state that changes recommendations (305-311).
- Option rows: large rounded cards with icon tile, title, one-line sub-caption, radio at right (306).
- Primary actions: full-width black pill at the bottom; secondary as grey text link ("Not now", "Reset").
- Popover menus: glassy, icons on the right or left, destructive item in red at the bottom (276, 333).
- Inline rename: tapping "Edit title" turns the heading into a text field (333-335).

Charts and data viz
- Three score rings as the day's headline (Strain/Recovery/Sleep); gradient arcs per domain (orange, green-lime, blue-purple); hatched segment shows today's latest contribution (296, 304, 325). Empty ring = grey with "-%".
- Personal-baseline viz everywhere: vertical range pill with marker (301), sparkline over a shaded normal band with glowing end dot (348), "Typical range" ticks on sleep stage rings (350).
- Before/after bars for load change (329), stacked zone bar with colour-coded labels (329), arc gauge 1-10 for effort (329), dial gauge for stress (297), segmented energy bar (297), dot-matrix macro grids (299).
- Trend table: period rows (3/7/14/30/90-day) with change % badge and a sparkline per row (338). Excellent compact trend summary.
- Hypnogram with scrub cursor and tooltip (351); HR line chart with zone label (329).
- "Receipt" calculation table: inputs, rule, bold total (358).

Typography and spacing
- One sans family; big date title (~28pt semibold), section headers ~20pt semibold, card titles ~17pt, numbers large (~28-34pt) with units at ~60% size in grey, captions grey ~13-15pt. Generous 16-24px padding, 12px gaps between cards.

Colour
- Neutral canvas; colour reserved for data meaning (domain colours, status colours). Orange used for alerts/celebration (banner 296, Finish pill 273, Reset 313, Lower status 301). Red only for destructive (276, 289 trash). Sleep pages switch to a dark navy theme (350-359).

Motion hints
- Top pill morphs Syncing -> Sync Complete (315). Insight card shows a spinner while regenerating (311). Collapsing header/tab bar on scroll. Rest-timer pill turns black while counting (273-275). Iridescent glow around AI cards suggests subtle animated shimmer (304, 325).

Copy tone
- Short, warm, second person, specific numbers: "Nice work getting in 2 sets of leg press in just 2 minutes 59 seconds" (296); "today is better suited to easy movement" (297). Explanations are plain and honest about limits: "Please allow up to 2 weeks for the app to learn" (346); "Strain rises logarithmically, not linearly" (339).

AI coach behaviour
- Appears as (1) an insight card under the headline rings, emoji + title + 3 lines, expand icon (296-317); (2) a glowing write-up on each detail page and each activity (325, 328, 347, 359); (3) a floating collapsed pill at the bottom of detail sheets that re-opens the write-up (329, 338, 350, 351); (4) the "Ask anything" bar; (5) "View insights" rows.
- Insights always cite the user's own numbers, compare to baseline or yesterday, give a verdict, and suggest an action. They regenerate when context changes (status change, 311).

Health data explained
- Every metric has: value + unit, status vs personal normal, a range viz, a source line ("Tracked with Apple Watch", 350), an (i) explainer with bands, trend table, method text and articles (338-345), and calculation breakdowns (358). New data types are announced once (315). Learning periods are stated (346).
