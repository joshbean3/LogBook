## 3. Patterns

- **Layout**: One hero metric per page (big number, sublabel, delta chip), then a status or confidence card, then a list. Generous whitespace, about 20-24pt side gutters, 12-16pt gaps between cards.
- **Navigation**: A floating pill tab bar (4 tabs) plus a separate floating round "+" for global quick-add (620-622). The AI ask bar floats just above the tab bar on every main tab. Detail views are card sheets that stack (549, 561). Action sheets come from the bottom with a back chevron when nested.
- **Bottom sheet anatomy (very consistent)**: grabber or back chevron, centered small grey title, content, one full-width black pill CTA at the bottom (549, 561, 603, 617). A destructive action is a red icon in a tinted circle at top right (549).
- **Entry input**: A big centered stepper with round grey -/+ buttons and the value in large bold type. The time row defaults to now. Presets beat typing: "Add 250 ml" next to "Add custom" (575).
- **Totals**: The day total sits right-aligned in grey on the section header; a target becomes "x/y" plus a mini ring (575).
- **Cards**: White, large radius (about 16-20pt), very soft shadow, sometimes a faint state tint (orange confidence card, 620). Lists are inset rounded rows with emoji or icon tiles at left and value + arrow at right.
- **Charts**: A cumulative line, this period vs last period (bright color against grey), with the latest point glowing (603). Tiny step sparklines per exercise ending in a glowing dot (617). A semicircle gauge with a tick ring, range labels at its ends, and a short segment from reference to actual (620-621). Ring icons per biomarker (622).
- **Comparison framing**: Always "vs last period": the two dates with colored dots matching the chart lines (603), and "from last week" chips (620-621).
- **Color as state**: The whole page gradient, number fill, sublabel and even the mascot shift warm (worse) or cool green (better) (620 vs 621). Orange is used for caution and confidence badges; red/coral for the current-period line; blue for "yes" checks and highlights.
- **Typography**: Hero numbers about 56-64pt bold with a gradient fill; H1 about 28pt bold; section headers about 18pt semibold; body 15-16pt; grey secondary text 13-14pt. Numbers dominate; labels are quiet.
- **Honesty about data quality**: A confidence % with a reason ("Partial or aging data"), "9 biomarkers missing", "No data available" rows, "Next update in 4 days", "updates weekly", and locked analyses with explicit unlock rules (576, 619-622). The app never fakes a number.
- **Unlocking via counts**: Tags show yes/no counts toward a 5 and 5 threshold; met counts go solid (576). This makes the "log more" ask concrete.
- **Onboarding for a feature**: A single intro card per feature with an illustration, a two-line value prop, a cadence note and a floating Next pill (619).
- **AI coach presence**: A persistent "Ask ... anything" bar with an animated-looking blob avatar whose color follows page state and which gets a red dot when it has something to say (620-622). It is not a separate tab.
- **Copy tone**: Short, plain, second person. "No logs yet. Once you add a log, it will show up here." The CTA verbs name the result ('Filter by "Active Duration"', "Add to contributions", "Tap to sync").
- **Motion hints**: Glow on the latest data point, gradient backgrounds, a stacked-sheet parallax (the parent card is visible behind), and a mascot that likely animates.
