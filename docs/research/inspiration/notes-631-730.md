## 3. Patterns

Layout and navigation
- Almost every detail and settings screen is a bottom sheet over the main tab (grab handle, rounded top, page behind peeks). Sub-pages push inside the sheet; choices and pickers open a second, shorter sheet stacked on top (675, 689, 698, 702). Three depth levels that never lose context.
- Floating capsule tab bar with a raised round "+" in the center for quick logging (667, 708). Floating secondary pill inside long detail sheets for section jumping (648, 649, 670).
- Collapsing header: big icon+title+value at top, collapses to centered small title with blur on scroll (649, 665).
- Settings grammar: section heading (small grey or semibold) -> grouped white card -> rows with pastel icon tile, title, grey subtitle, chevron (673). Destructive/maintenance actions as separate full-width pills with centered text, delete in red-on-pink (658, 660).
- Explicit Save: disabled (pale) until a change is made, then dark/active (660, 688, 698-699, 702-704). Success confirmed by a green top toast with exact timestamp (700, 708).
- Footnotes under cards explain consequences or rules in one plain sentence (649, 688, 701, 706).

Cards and data viz
- Metric header formula: icon + name, huge value with unit, date under it, status on the right ("Decreasing", "Normal range 12-71%") (648, 670).
- Chart conventions: right-side y axis labels only, few gridlines, solid line for actual and dotted for projection (641, 648), shaded personal-normal band (670), dotted average line with pill label (670), emphasized latest point with glow (648, 670), labeled start/end chips (641), dual-series charts (line over bars, 648).
- Range control: < [1M 3M 6M 1Y] [calendar] > segmented pill (648, 670). Metric chips switch series within one chart (670).
- Trend table with tiny sparklines per period (3/7/14/30/90-day) and "based on 7-day rolling averages" (649).
- Ring trio as the daily summary (667, 708, 712-713); capsule vertical gauges for vitals (712-713); half-arc gauges for goal progress (714, 715); waffle/dot grids for composition (714, 715); segmented battery bars for energy (667, 708, 715); diverging gradient bar for net balance (715); segmented breakdown bar with thresholds legend (670).
- Freshness labels everywhere: "Last updated at 9.24 PM", "Last data point on...", "6m ago" (649, 667, 712).
- Empty states keep the full structure and show dashes or 0 with grey rings, never a blank (707, 708 nutrition 0g, 712).

Typography, spacing, color
- Scale roughly: hero numerals 40-60pt (635 huge frosted numerals), metric value ~34pt bold, section headings ~20pt semibold, row titles 17pt, subtitles 14-15pt grey, footnotes 13pt grey. Generous 16px gutters, ~12px gaps between cards, 16-20px card radius.
- Base is near-white lavender-grey background with white cards; color reserved for data (each metric owns one hue: strain orange, recovery green, sleep blue-purple, fat blue, carbs orange, protein pink). Primary buttons are black pills; links grey. Orange used as the highlight for the "your" number (635).
- Light and dark are both full designs; theme switches instantly in place (663, 666).

Motion hints
- Custom loader that shows what is being analyzed (day pills sliding with rings, 677); live countdown in a disabled button (711); drag lift with shadow (703); toasts sliding in at top (700, 708); glow around hero card and floating pills.

Copy tone
- Second person, short, warm but plain: "Let's improve that!", "Check your inbox!", "Please wait while we determine your optimal sleep goal." Settings subtitles state exactly when something happens ("when an exercise is logged to Apple Health").

AI coach
- Appears as a titled paragraph attached directly under the ring trio on Home (667 "Sleep & Recovery Check-in", 708 "Prioritizing Recovery"), with emoji icon, truncation and an expand icon. It cites the time, today's number, compares to the user's usual, connects to the user's goal and gives one directive. Has its own settings entry ("Intelligence", 673). Goals can be "automatically calculated" from 90 days of data (675-677) = the AI/algorithm proposing a target the user accepts.

Health data explained
- Bio age: one sentence with the gap in years and a two-marker gauge (635); projection phrased as a sentence with tappable variables (641); learn-more article carousel (641).
- Personal normal ranges, not population ones (670 band, 708 "lower than your usual").
- Data provenance is user-controllable: per-metric source list with priority order and hide toggles, plus the conflict rule in plain words (698-705); latency disclosed (706); history window adjustable with a load-time warning (688).

## 4. For Logbook

Today
- Put a ring trio at the top under the habit buttons: e.g. Readiness (from HRV/resting HR), Sleep, Training load for the day, each in one owned hue on Columbia Blue cards; tap a ring -> metric sheet (667, 708, 670).
- Assistant "check-in" paragraph right under the rings, titled by situation ("Easy day suggested"), with time, today's number vs your usual, link to the race goal, one directive, expand to full text; "Apply" for any proposed plan change (708, 667).
- Date in the header as a dropdown to view another day (667). Status chip (e.g. "Training / Taper / Sick / Travel - until changed") that the assistant and plan respect (667).
- Green top toast "Synced with Health at 6:12 AM" after Health or Strava sync (700, 708).
- Floating capsule tab bar with a center "+" for quick log (run note, lift, learned item, journal) is tempting, but Logbook has six tabs; consider five visible + center add or keep six with a separate floating add button (667).

Goals
- Goal cards that show the target value large with a small illustration, grouped by domain headings (674).
- When creating a goal, a "Choose a method" sheet: Set manually (stepper) vs Let the assistant suggest (based on last 90 days of runs/sleep), with a content-specific loading state ("Looking at your last 12 weeks of mileage") (675-677).
- Half-arc gauges for weekly/monthly progress toward a number (714).

Learned
- Empty days keep their slot with a dash rather than disappearing, so the month archive reads as a grid (712).
- Resources-style horizontal carousel for "related past learnings" at the bottom of an entry (641).

Plan
- Morning-schedule card can borrow the timeline widget: a horizontal time axis with sleep block and workout markers (713).
- Per-session sheets with metric chips (Pace / HR / Distance) switching one chart (670).
- Status/phase chip ("Base / Build / Taper") at top (667).

Progress
- Metric sheet template: icon+name, big value, date, status word on the right, chart with right-side axis, solid actual + dotted projection, personal-normal band, average line pill, emphasized latest point, 1M/3M/6M/1Y + calendar control (648, 670).
- Mileage: weekly bars with dotted target line and a trophy marker on weeks the target was hit (714).
- Trend table: 7/14/30/90-day change with tiny sparklines and "based on rolling averages" footnote; works for weekly mileage, resting HR, weight (649).
- Race projection as a sentence with tappable variables: "In [8 weeks] at this trend your half marathon is [1:42]" with projection line vs goal line (641).
- Training load or recovery as a segmented battery bar (667, 715); load breakdown bar Low/Normal/High with thresholds (670).
- Freshness label on each card ("Last Strava activity 2h ago") (649, 712).

Journal
- Daily end-of-day reminder as an explained notification toggle (672).
- "Remember when" card could use the reveal style for milestones (big number moment, share/next) sparingly, e.g. anniversary of a PR (635).

Assistant
- Speaks in short sentences with numbers versus personal baseline (708). Proposals arrive as cards with one-sentence reason and Apply/Dismiss; mirror the "Automatically calculate" pattern where the assistant computes and the user accepts (675-677). Give it a settings page for tone/frequency (673).

Settings
- One tall sheet; sub-pages push inside; pickers open small stacked sheets with Done (658, 688-689).
- Grouped cards with pastel icon tiles and subtitles (673). Separate "Customization" group: Goals, Units, HR zones, Calculations, Data window (673).
- Data sources page for Health and Strava: list sources per metric, numbered priority with drag reorder, hide via eye toggle, rule in one footnote, Save disabled until change (698-705).
- Notifications: master toggle reveals grouped options, each with when-it-fires explanation (671-672).
- Appearance with visual preview tiles and instant switch (663, 666). Maintenance actions (Clear cache, Export logs, Restore) as separate full-width pills at the bottom with version number (658).
- Account: derived values shown read-only (age from birthday), destructive in red-on-pale fill with system confirm (660, 662).

Onboarding
- Single-question screens with a big centered input and bottom pinned Continue (710). Sign-in with Apple primary and email as text link (661). Magic link wait screen with countdown resend (711).
- Health connection page modeled on Manage CGM: connected card with Disconnect and latency note, empty state with Connect (706-707).
- First Health import: reveal moment for a headline number (e.g. "You ran 1,240 km this year") with share and Next (635).

Widgets (later)
- Ring trio, today's run/plan, and mileage-vs-target widgets; empty versions with dashes (712-715).
